The Venue

Light, height, and 2,800 square feet to work with.

The Alcove sits on the second floor at 358 Speers Road, spanning 2,800 square feet of open space. High ceilings and a full run of windows keep the room bright straight through the afternoon — the kind of light that flatters a head table and a camera roll in equal measure.

The finish is deliberately quiet: pale walls, wood floors, white-painted structure overhead and brass fixtures. It reads elegant on its own, and it takes a theme without arguing with it.

Everything in the room is ours, which means the tables, the chairs, the runners and the styling are already here on your date. You bring the food, the guest list and the people who matter.

One address, one price, one setup — instead of five vendors and a rental truck.

On site

What's already in the building.

  • Kitchenette & prep room

    Newly renovated, with fridges, freezers, microwaves, a dishwasher and warming equipment. No stove or oven — catering arrives ready to serve.

  • Sound system & microphone

    Included with every rental. DJs are welcome to bring their own rig — tell us what you need in advance and we'll have it ready.

  • Accessible washrooms

    Women's, men's and a fully accessible washroom, all on the same floor as the event space.

  • Free on-site parking

    At the building, at no extra cost, first come first served. No meters, no validation, no hunting down Speers Road.

  • Furniture & décor included

    Tables, cross-back chairs, runners, centrepieces, signage and backdrops come with the package — they are not a line item you negotiate later.

  • Your caterer, your vendors

    Outside food and beverage are welcome. Vendors are reviewed and approved ahead of the date, and alcohol is fine with a permit.

How a booking works

  1. Send your date

    Fill in the inquiry form or email us. We reply within 48 hours with availability and a quote.

  2. Hold it with a deposit

    A 50% non-refundable deposit confirms the date, alongside a refundable $250 security deposit returned after the event if the space comes back undamaged.

  3. Plan the room

    On The Elevated and The Experience, we walk through your styling on a design call and confirm your vendors ahead of the day.

  4. Settle and celebrate

    The balance is due five days before the event. We set the room before you arrive. Bookings are final — if plans change we'll help you reschedule, with a change fee.

Payment: e-transfer and cash only at this time.

About

A note from us.

We are a husband-and-wife team behind The Alcove. The venue came out of a shared love of bringing people together and celebrating life's meaningful moments.

Inspired by Shez's passion for event planning — and our own experience planning milestones like our wedding, bridal and baby showers — we understood how much it matters to find a venue that feels elegant and affordable at once, without compromising on style or budget.

With that in mind we created The Alcove: a one-stop-shop event venue designed to make celebrations effortless. Our goal is to take the stress out of planning and help bring your vision to life, so every celebration feels personal, intentional and beautifully done.

The Alcove is more than a venue to us — it's a reflection of our belief that life's moments deserve to be celebrated with joy, ease and affordability.

— Az & Shez

Questions

Before you ask.

Where are you located, and is there parking?

358 Speers Road, Unit 204, Oakville, Ontario L6K 3R9, on the second floor. There is free on-site parking, first come first served.

What is the maximum capacity?

100 people at any given time.

How do we secure our booking?

Reach us through the inquiry form or at info@thealcoveoakville.com. A 50% non-refundable deposit secures the date, and the balance is due five days before the event.

Are the time slots fixed, or are you flexible?

Full-day bookings run 11:00am to midnight. Half days are either 11:00am to 4:00pm for social events, or 6:00pm to midnight for evenings. Additional time is available on request and may carry a fee.

Is there a security deposit?

Yes — a refundable $250 security deposit is required at booking, on top of the rental fee. It is returned once the venue is handed back undamaged.

Do we get extra time to set up and clean up?

Setup and cleanup are included within your rental slot. If you need more time, ask us in advance — additional hours may carry a fee.

What is your cancellation policy?

All bookings are final and non-refundable. If your plans change, we will do our best to help you reschedule, with a change fee applied.

Is there a kitchenette or prep room?

Yes — a newly renovated kitchenette and prep room with fridges, freezers, microwaves, a dishwasher and warming equipment. There is no stove or oven on site, so food should arrive prepared. Catering is arranged by you.

Are there washrooms on site?

Yes — women's, men's and an accessible washroom.

Can we bring outside food, drinks and vendors?

Outside food and beverage are welcome. Vendors need to be approved ahead of your date, and serving alcohol requires a permit.

Can we decorate the space ourselves?

Décor is included in every package and can be customized to your event. Outside vendors are welcome after a quick review. We do not allow balloons, silly string, confetti, or tape on the walls.

What AV equipment is available?

A sound system with a microphone is included in the venue rental. DJs are welcome to bring their own equipment. Let us know your AV needs in advance.
